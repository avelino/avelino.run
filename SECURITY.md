# Security Policy

This repo is my personal site, avelino.run: the Hugo source, the Netlify functions behind ActivityPub and the newsletter signup, and the GitHub workflows that keep it updated.

## Reporting a vulnerability

Please don't open a public issue. Report it privately through GitHub: go to the [Security tab](https://github.com/avelino/avelino.run/security/advisories/new) and open a draft advisory.

Tell me what you found and how to reproduce it. I'll reply as soon as I can.

## Scope

In scope: the Netlify functions in `netlify/functions`, the workflows and scripts in `.github`, and anything that could leak a secret or let someone publish to the site or the newsletter. Typos, broken links and content opinions are not security issues, a regular issue is fine for those.
